Alex
Kantrowitz

Alex Kantrowitz is a veteran journalist who's interviewed some of the world's top CEOs, including Sam Altman, Demis Hassabis, Dario Amodei, Larry Ellison, and Mark Zuckerberg. His company, Big Technology, serves more than 150,000 newsletter subscribers and registers millions of annual podcast downloads, delivering accessible news about tech, business, and AI. He's also an on-air contributor at CNBC and the author of Always Day One: How The Tech Titans Plan To Stay On Top Forever. Alex graduated from Cornell University's School of Industrial and Labor Relations.
